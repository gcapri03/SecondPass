Relevel

A quiet coach for the moments that matter on camera. Relevel learns your own best expression, then gives you a discreet nudge the moment you drift from it — in interviews, meetings, and everywhere in between.

Visit the Relevel site

How it works

  • Calibrate once

    Save a few expressions that represent you at your most composed — for your laptop, tablet, or phone.

  • Get a discreet cue

    If your expression drifts while your camera's on, Relevel gives you a small, private signal to reset — never a banner, never a sound.

  • Nothing leaves your device

    All facial analysis happens on-device. Second Pass never sees, stores, or transmits your facial data, and it never touches the cloud.

Relevel is available for desktop, tablet, and mobile. For product details, pricing, and to download the app, visit relevel.app.
