DoomSquirrel

Help and support

Thanks for playing DoomSquirrel! You'll find quick answers below. If you're still stuck, write to us at hello@secondpass.me and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

How to play

  1. Memorize the picture we show you.
  2. Scroll the feed and tap that exact picture.
  3. Faster finds earn more stars. A wrong tap adds 2 seconds to your time.

Easy shows one big column. Medium and Hard pack in more pictures and sneakier look-alikes, and Hard doesn't show a reminder of the picture while you scroll. On Medium and Hard, pictures you've already tapped by mistake stay marked with an X, so you can rule them out.

If time runs out, you'll hear a little bonk (with sound on) and the feed shows where the picture was. Your results turn green when you find it and pink when time runs out.

Stars and hints

You earn up to 3 stars each round, based on your time. Spend them on hints: Radar shows roughly how far down the feed the picture is, on every level, and on Hard, Peek shows the picture again for a moment. Rounds where you use a hint can earn up to 2 stars. You can also watch an optional video ad for 3 bonus stars.

Picture packs

Squirrels and Shapes are free. Cats and Sweets unlock with stars, or you can buy them once. Your stars live on your phone, so deleting the app resets them unless you've signed in with Apple (see below). Packs you bought can be restored on the same Apple ID with Restore purchases.

Saving your progress

Want to keep your stars and best times if you get a new phone, or play on your iPad too? Tap Sign in with Apple on the home screen card, or Save progress with Apple at the bottom of the home screen. It's optional, and we don't ask Apple for your name or email.

On a new device, open DoomSquirrel and sign in with the same Apple Account. The game keeps the best of both: your highest star balance and your fastest times.

Deleting your account

If you've signed in with Apple, tap Delete account at the bottom of the home screen, then confirm. This deletes your saved progress, friends list and challenge inbox, and your name on past leaderboards changes to "Former player." The game then starts fresh on this phone. Packs you bought aren't lost: tap Restore purchases to get them back. Our privacy policy has the details.

Restoring purchases

New phone or reinstalled the game? On the home screen, tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy the pack.

Refunds for purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Friend challenges

After a round without hints, tap Challenge friends to share a 6-character code. Everyone who enters the code plays the exact same feed, and the fastest time tops the leaderboard. Each player gets one try, and leaving a challenge partway through counts as did not finish, so take a breath before you start!

My challenges and friends

Tap My challenges on the home screen to see every challenge you've created or joined. A number on the button means something new is waiting: a challenge from a friend, or new results on one of yours.

  • Waiting for you lists friends' challenges you haven't played yet.
  • Challenges you've played show your place. Green means you're in first, pink means someone beat you or you didn't finish, and Waiting for friends means nobody else has played yet. Tap See results to open the leaderboard.
  • When you play someone's challenge, or they play yours, you become friends in the game, and their new challenges show up under Waiting for you without a text.
  • To stop someone's challenges, tap Show friends in My challenges and remove them, or use the ••• button next to their name on a leaderboard to hide them.

If you haven't signed in with Apple, friends are tied to this copy of the game, so deleting and reinstalling it starts your friends list fresh. Play a challenge with them again to reconnect. Signing in with Apple keeps you connected on any device where you sign in.

Reminders

In My challenges, you can turn on reminders to get a nudge when friends' challenges are waiting. You'll get at most one a day, in the evening, and it's created on your phone. To stop them, tap Turn off reminders at the bottom of My challenges, or turn off notifications for DoomSquirrel in your iPhone's Settings.

Challenge codes

If a code doesn't work, check for mix-ups like the number 0 and the letter O. Codes don't use those, or the number 1 and the letter I, to keep things simple.

Ads and privacy

Ads keep DoomSquirrel free. You'll see a banner on menus and a full-screen ad every few rounds, between rounds rather than during play.

To change how your data is used for ads, tap Privacy choices at the bottom of the home screen, if it's shown for your region. You can also stop ad tracking for every app in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Our privacy policy explains what the game collects. If you've signed in with Apple, you can delete your account in the game (see Deleting your account above). Otherwise, to have your challenge results, friends list or inbox deleted, email us the challenge codes you played and the name you used.

Accessibility

DoomSquirrel works with VoiceOver and follows your Reduce Motion setting. The game itself is a visual matching game, so it depends on sight. If something gets in your way, please tell us. We read what you send and do our best to reply.
