How we work

  • One app, one job

    We'd rather ship something narrow and dependable than something broad and forgettable.

  • Your data stays yours

    Where it's technically possible, we process on your device instead of in our cloud. If we don't need to see something, we don't build a way to see it.

  • Built to last a second look

    We hold every release to the same standard: would we be comfortable if a careful person examined it closely? That's the bar, not the finish line.
