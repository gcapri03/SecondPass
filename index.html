How we work

  • One app, one job

    We'd rather ship something narrow and dependable than something broad and forgettable.

  • We tell you what each app collects

    Some apps are built to keep everything on your device. Others may collect standard information, like account details or basic usage data, to work properly. Either way, each app's own privacy policy says exactly what it collects and why.

  • Built to last a second look

    We hold every release to the same standard: would we be comfortable if a careful person examined it closely? That's the bar, not the finish line.
