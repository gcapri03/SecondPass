About

A small studio, on purpose

Second Pass LLC is an independent software studio based in Miami, Florida. We design and build a small family of apps, each one narrow enough to be excellent at a single thing.

Why "Second Pass"

The best work rarely survives its first draft. A second pass is where a manuscript becomes readable, where a design becomes usable, where a rough idea becomes something worth someone's time. That's the standard we hold our software to before it reaches you — and the reason for the name.

What we build

We build apps for people who'd rather own a tool that does one job well than a platform that tries to do everything. Our current focus is on-device, privacy-first software for professional and personal use — starting with Relevel.

Get in touch

Questions about a Second Pass app, a partnership, or anything else — we read every note. Reach us at hello@secondpass.co.
