About

A small studio, on purpose

Second Pass LLC is an independent software studio based in Miami, Florida. We design and build a small family of apps, each one narrow enough to be excellent at a single thing.

Why "Second Pass"

The best work rarely survives its first draft. A second pass is where a manuscript becomes readable, where a design becomes usable, where a rough idea becomes something worth someone's time. That's the standard we hold our software to before it reaches you, and the reason for the name.

What we build

We build apps for people who'd rather own a tool that does one job well than a platform that tries to do everything. Our first app is deep in development, and we're keeping the details close until it's ready to launch. Not every app we build will work the same way; where an app does need to collect information, its own privacy policy will say so plainly.

Get in touch

Questions about a Second Pass app, a partnership, or anything else? We read every note. Reach us at hello@secondpass.me.
