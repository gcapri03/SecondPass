Second Pass LLC ("Second Pass," "we," "us") builds a small family of software products, including Relevel. This policy explains how we handle information across our company website and, at a summary level, across our apps. Each app may publish its own, more detailed privacy policy covering how that specific app works — where one exists, it governs that app.

What we collect on this website

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics beyond what's needed to keep it running securely. If you email us, we keep that correspondence to respond to you and for our own records.

What our apps collect

Our apps are built around a simple principle: if we don't need to see something, we don't build a way to see it. Where an app processes sensitive information — such as Relevel's on-device facial analysis — that processing happens on your device and is never uploaded to us. Any account information an app requires (such as an email address, sign-in method, or purchase history) is limited to what's needed to manage your plan and entitlements.

How we use information

We use the limited information we do collect to operate our apps and this website, respond to you, meet legal obligations, and improve what we build. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers that help us operate (for example, payment processing or cloud infrastructure for account and billing data — never for on-device data like facial analysis), or when required by law.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion, subject to what the law requires us to keep.

Children

Our products are not directed to children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know directly.
