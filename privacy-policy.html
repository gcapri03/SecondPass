Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Second Pass LLC ("Second Pass," "we," "us") builds a small family of software apps. This policy explains how we handle personal information on our company website, secondpass.me, and sets the company-wide baseline for our apps. Because each app works differently, each app publishes its own privacy policy describing what it collects, whether it uses analytics, advertising, or tracking, and who it's intended for. Where an app has its own policy, that policy governs the app and controls if it conflicts with this one.

This website

Hosting and fonts. Like most websites, this site is served by a hosting provider and loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you visit, those providers automatically receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the page requested, to deliver the site, keep it secure, and maintain their services. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Email. If you email us, we keep the correspondence to respond to you and for our records.

Our apps

What each app collects depends on what it does, and its own privacy policy and app store privacy label describe it. Depending on the app, that may include:

  • Information you provide, such as an email address, a display or player name, or content you create.
  • Identifiers, such as an account ID, an anonymous player ID, or a device advertising identifier.
  • Purchase records, such as what you bought and a store receipt. Payments are processed by the app store; we don't receive your card or bank details.
  • Device, usage, diagnostic, and approximate location information (for example, derived from an IP address).
  • Information collected by third-party analytics, advertising, sign-in, or purchase services that an app uses, including cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies that may track activity across apps and websites where you permit it.

Some of our apps process certain information on your device, so it isn't sent to us. If an app uses your camera, microphone, or images or recordings of your face or voice, its privacy policy will say what is processed, where, and whether anything leaves your device. Before collecting biometric identifiers or information, we give any notice and obtain any consent the law requires.

How we use information

We use personal information to provide, operate, and maintain our website and apps; process and restore purchases; respond to you and provide support; send messages you've asked for; show and measure ads in apps that include them; understand usage and improve our products; detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security, and technical issues; enforce our Terms of Service; and comply with legal obligations.

How we share information

We share personal information in the following ways:

  • Service providers that run parts of our website and apps for us, such as hosting, cloud databases, email, sign-in, analytics, purchase management, and app stores. We permit them to use it to provide their services to us, except as their own policies allow for services like advertising networks.
  • Advertising partners in apps that show ads, as described in that app's privacy policy.
  • Other users, where a feature is designed to be shared, such as a leaderboard.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law or legal process, or when we believe in good faith it's needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of Second Pass, our users, or others, or to prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

We don't sell personal information for money. Some laws treat sharing information with advertising partners for personalized ads as a "sale" or "sharing." Where an app does that, its privacy policy says so and explains how to opt out.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request a copy, ask us to correct or delete it, or opt out of personalized advertising or similar processing. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them as required. To protect you, we may need to verify your request; because some apps don't use accounts, we may ask for details (such as a player name or challenge code) that let us find your information. Exercising your rights won't affect how we treat you, except as the law allows. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings. To make a request, email hello@secondpass.me.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy and in each app's policy, including to provide the service, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations. Then we delete or de-identify it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children and age

Each app states its intended audience and any minimum age in its own privacy policy and app store listing. Unless an app's policy says otherwise, our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without the parental consent the law requires. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Where information is processed

Second Pass is based in the United States. We and our service providers may store and process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws than where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the date at the top. If we make a material change, we'll give notice as appropriate, such as on this site or in the affected app.
